Darts Caller – Recording Script

For Oche, a free voice-controlled darts scoring app. 393 lines, about 12–15 minutes of finished audio.

How to record
  • Style: an energetic darts caller / MC in a hall – your own style. Please do not imitate any specific real referee or caller.
  • Dry, close-mic recording: no reverb, no music, no effects, no compression pumping. Same distance to the mic throughout.
  • WAV, mono, 44.1 or 48 kHz, 24-bit. One or several files, named in order (part1.wav, part2.wav …).
  • Read the lines in this order. Do not read the line numbers.
  • Leave about 1.5 seconds of silence between lines.
  • Flubbed a line? Pause, then simply say it again – we keep the last take. No need to edit.
  • Lines marked “× 3 takes”: say them three times with a pause in between, each a little different.
  • Numbers are read British style: “one hundred and forty”. 0 is “No score!”.
